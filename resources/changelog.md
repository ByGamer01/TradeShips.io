📦 **TradeShips v0.3.0 — Port prosperity and submarine rebalance**

⚓ **Submarines**

- Submarines now hunt only military ships: warships, frigates and carriers
- Trade ships are safe from torpedoes again
- Hidden enemy submarines no longer reveal their position through a floating health bar — you only see them (periscope and health) inside your fleet's detection range

💰 **Port prosperity**

- Trade routes earn extra gold for every level of the destination port
- Upgrading a port past level 500 grants a one-time prosperity reward: all debt forgiven plus 100M gold
- Nation bots now invest in port upgrades to chase prosperity

🎮 **Lobbies**

- Public lobbies rotate between FFA, team and special game configurations

📦 **TradeShips v0.2.0 — Congress and war economy**

🏆 **Combined global mission**

- Maritime victories now require both 100% control of the central sea route and 500M gold without debt
- The full objective is always visible below the fleet and building bar
- Trade ships no longer contribute control power

🏛️ **World Congress**

- New optional World Congress game mode with timed voting sessions
- Players vote on temporary sanctions, nuclear restrictions, infrastructure modifiers, and open-seas rules
- Dedicated voting panel with synchronized multiplayer results

💰 **War economy**

- Attacking progressively drains gold; peace lets the economy recover
- Conquests provide a limited recovery reward, and players can request gold from allies
- Fleet upkeep can create debt and bankruptcy when expenses exceed GDP
- Debt and bankruptcy are visible in the leaderboard and player panel

⚓ **Fleet and combat**

- Aircraft carriers launch visible aircraft against enemy fleets, including other carriers
- Warships fire thin shell tracers; submarines exclusively launch torpedoes
- Naval mines destroy hostile ships, while anti-air frigates only engage aircraft
- Unit death effects now match the destroyed naval class

🤖 **AI, teams, and interface**

- Nations use mixed fleets, naval buildings, Congress, and economic strategy
- Team games fill empty player slots with nations until humans join
- Public lobbies now allow 10 seconds to join
- Buildings and vehicles are separated in the HUD with reorganized hotkeys

📦 **TradeShips v0.1.0 — First alpha**

⚓ **Maritime objective**

- Win by controlling the central sea route, not by conquering territory
- FFA: an open-water trade-lane corridor marked with a flag and a glowing lane
- Teams: a circular zone in the open sea; your whole team's fleet adds up
- Rankings now show maritime control instead of territorial conquest

💰 **Trade economy**

- Tariffs against enemy trade, unlocked by building a Congress
- Merchant routes with visible value, risk, and escorts in the trade panel
- Trade bonus between allies

🚢 **Fleet**

- New naval units: Submarine, Anti-Air Frigate, Aircraft Carrier, Naval Mine
- Trimmed arsenal: no nukes and no SAMs — wars are decided at sea
- Nations now contest the maritime objective with fleets of their own

🗺️ **Maps**

- Public rotation limited to maritime maps (islands, archipelagos, and straits)
- Nations always play at least Hard difficulty
